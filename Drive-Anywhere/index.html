About the game

No track. No limits.

Drive Anywhere is built around the freedom to spawn vehicles, get behind the wheel, and travel across an endlessly generated world. Switch between third-person and first-person views, look around inside the vehicle, and explore without a fixed route.

The world is also yours to shape. Build ramps, roads, tracks, and other structures to create stunt areas, test vehicles, or connect the places you discover.
