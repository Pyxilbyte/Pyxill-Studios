About the game

The world has depth.

Vertex Survival combines stylized low-poly terrain with true underground exploration. The surface forms into recognizable biomes and mountains while caves twist beneath it using marching-cubes terrain generation.

Explore above and below ground, gather what the landscape provides, and prepare to survive a world that can open in any direction.
