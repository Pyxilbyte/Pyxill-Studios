About the game

Every horizon leads somewhere new.

Infinite Horizon is a survival sandbox built around exploration and freedom. Forests, plains, mountains, caves, and resources continue beyond the edge of the map while the player chooses where to travel and what to build.

Craft tools, manage survival needs, cook food, build structures, and defend yourself when night changes the world.
