About the game

The ordinary feels wrong.

Door 47 builds tension through atmosphere instead of obvious scares. Explore an apparently normal place, notice changes, uncover clues, and slowly learn why Door 47 refuses to stay closed—or stay in one place.

The story rewards careful players who pay attention to rooms, sounds, objects, and details that are easy to dismiss.
